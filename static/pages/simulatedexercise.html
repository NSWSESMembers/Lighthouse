Talk to the simulated teams on the exercise radio. It's best to join from a phone, so scan this code with your phone's camera. You can also open .

To join, you need the exercise's join code. For an individual exercise it's shown in CADence. For a group exercise, your facilitator gives it to you.

What changes

While an exercise is showing, its simulated vehicles and vessels take the place of real radio positions in every Lighthouse tab on :

  • LAD: they move on the map, match their Beacon teams and appear in Instant Task suggestions. A map alert shows while they're on.
  • Job page: the Nearest Asset Locations map shows them for the job's own headquarters, with a note under the map.
  • Situational awareness map: a Simulated Exercise Assets layer appears under Lighthouse > NSW SES.

This lasts until you show real radio positions again, the exercise ends or you close Chrome. Satellite trackers still show as normal, and production Beacon is never affected. The exercise list and positions come from the CADence exercise service, which checks your Beacon sign-in.
